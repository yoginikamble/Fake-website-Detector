How the Security Analyzer Works

A multi-layered rule-based inspection pipeline executed in milliseconds

1. Lexical & Syntax

Evaluates URL length, '@' masking symbols, double slashes, hyphen spam, and suspicious keywords.

2. SSRF Protection

Blocks private RFC1918 subnets, localhost, loopback, and link-local destinations to protect the scanner host.

3. SSL & Transport

Probes live HTTPS encryption, certificates, non-standard ports, and multiple redirect hops safely.

4. Transparent Score

Aggregates weighted indicator points into an explainable 0–100 risk score and actionable safety advice.
